 Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it and how you stay in control. By using {CONFIG.appName}, you agree to this policy.

 1. Who we are
 GaoninGo is operated by Udaybhan Gupta (Smart Pay Kirana Store). This policy explains what personal information we collect through the app, why we collect it, and the choices you have.
 
 2. Information we handle
 The app collects the mobile number you enter and an authentication token. An address includes a label (such as home, work, or other), the recipient's name and phone number, and the address details.
 
 3. Location information and permissions
 If you choose to use your current location, the app requests foreground location permission, reads your current position, and may ask your device's location service for a readable place name. Your coordinates may be sent with product searches and listings, or when you save an address. The app does not request background location permission or track your location continuously. You can turn location off at any time in your phone settings.
 
 4. How we use information
 We use your information to look up your account, maintain your session, find products and check delivery availability, manage your addresses, place and retrieve orders, and show order-status updates.
 
 5. Cart, orders, and delivery
 The cart is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. Order requests include an address identifier and the product, seller, and item details. The app processes the order total, status, and     delivery information returned by our API.
 
 6. Payments
 This app currently supports cash on delivery (COD) only.
 
 7. Sharing with sellers and delivery partners.
 We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with sellers and delivery partners to fulfill your orders.
 
 8. Third-party service providers
 The app uses the following third-party tools: Expo Location, Expo SecureStore, AsyncStorage, and the Socket.IO client.
 
 9. Data security
 We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your account is protected by authentication tokens, and your data is stored on secure servers.
 
 10. Data retention and deletion
 We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting our support team.
 
 11. Cookies and device storage
 The app uses AsyncStorage to store the cart, the selected address identifier, and the web authentication token.
 
 12. User rights and consent withdrawal
 You can deny or change location permission in your device settings, and delete saved addresses in the app. You can also ask us to show, correct, or delete your personal information.
 
 13. Changes to this policy
 If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app.
